Preview notes

Loading example…

Configure one to five physical strips above; their LED indices form one continuous WLED address range. Same direction keeps every row left-to-right. Alternating direction reverses each wrapped row without adding a connector. Snake uses that alternating flow with rounded connectors between adjacent rows while keeping the total LED count unchanged. Strip gap adds spacing at strip boundaries. These are browser approximations of WLED effect motion. Built-in WLED 16 palettes are included; exact random timing, hardware color order and custom palettes can differ. Exposure brightens the whole preview. Gamma lift makes dim LED dots more visible, even at very low preset brightness, while glow stays tied to the selected brightness. Brightness overrides affect this preview only. Effect speed adjusts motion without changing playlist durations; 1× follows the WLED speed setting, with effect-specific approximations.
