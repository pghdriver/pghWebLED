Preview notes

Loading example…

Configure one to five physical strips above; their LED indices form one continuous WLED address range. Line direction can alternate the flow within each strip; a new strip starts left to right. Line gap controls wrapped lines, and Strip gap adds spacing at strip boundaries. These are browser approximations of WLED effect motion. Built-in WLED 16 palettes are included; exact random timing, hardware color order and custom palettes can differ. Exposure brightens the whole preview. Gamma lift makes dim LED dots more visible, even at very low preset brightness, while glow stays tied to the selected brightness. Brightness overrides affect this preview only. Effect speed adjusts motion without changing playlist durations; 1× follows the WLED speed setting, with effect-specific approximations.
